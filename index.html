Privacy Policy

Application: MemoZen (Diary — Journeys — Memories)
Package Name: com.venhdev.memozen
Effective Date: October 19, 2026
Privacy Commitment: MemoZen is designed with an identity-first, privacy-conscious philosophy. We treat your personal journals, voice recordings, and journey memories as confidential sanctuary. We do not sell your personal data or profile you for advertising.

1. Introduction

This Privacy Policy explains how MemoZen ("we", "our", or "the App") collects, uses, processes, and protects your information when you access or use our mobile application on Android and iOS.

2. Information We Collect and Permissions Requested

To provide personal diary recording, collaborative journeys, and memory tracking, the App requests specific device permissions strictly when needed:

Permission / Data Android System Scope Purpose & Usage
Camera CAMERA To capture photos and visual moments directly attached to your memos and journey timelines.
Microphone RECORD_AUDIO To record intentional voice notes and audio memos upon your explicit tap.
Location (Foreground Only) ACCESS_FINE_LOCATION
ACCESS_COARSE_LOCATION		 To automatically geotag places to your journey notes and memories. Location is accessed strictly while the app is active in the foreground. Background location tracking is strictly disabled.
Photos & Media READ_MEDIA_IMAGES To allow you to choose existing photos or videos from your library to attach to your journal.
Notifications POST_NOTIFICATIONS To deliver intentional reminders (focus alerts, end-of-day reflection reminders) configured by you.
Biometrics USE_BIOMETRIC Optional local app lock (FaceID / Fingerprint) to guard your private sanctuary. Biometric data never leaves your device KeyStore.

Account & Authentication Data

When you register or sign in (via Google Sign-In or email credentials), we collect:

  • Your name and email address for account authentication and multi-device synchronization.
  • Your profile picture (if provided via Google Sign-In) to display in collaborative group journeys.

3. How We Use Your Information

We use the data collected strictly for the following purposes:

  • To create, edit, organize, and synchronize your memos, journeys, and tasks across your authorized devices.
  • To enable real-time collaborative journey timelines and expense settlements with your authorized group members.
  • To maintain system integrity, authenticate sessions, and revoke lost or unauthorized devices.

4. Third-Party Service Providers

We only share data with essential third-party service providers required for reliable infrastructure operation:

  • Google Play Services & Google Sign-In: For secure authentication and account linking.
  • Firebase Cloud Messaging (Google LLC): To deliver system push notifications.
  • Cloud Infrastructure & Object Storage: Secure cloud servers with encrypted storage for media attachments and database synchronization.

We do not integrate any third-party behavioral advertisement SDKs, data brokers, or marketing tracking networks.

5. Data Retention, Security, and Encryption

  • In-Transit: All data transmitted between the App and our backend services is encrypted using Transport Layer Security (TLS/HTTPS).
  • At-Rest: User tokens and credentials stored locally on your device are safeguarded using secure hardware-backed storage (Android Keystore / iOS Keychain).
  • Retention: Your journal data is retained as long as your account exists or until you choose to delete individual items.

6. User Rights & Data Deletion (Google Play Compliance)

You have full ownership of your data. You have the right to request deletion of your account and all associated personal data at any time:

  • In-App Deletion: You can sign out or request account closure directly within the Profile & Settings menu.
  • Direct Deletion Request: You can submit an email request to our support team at support@memozen.io with the subject line "Account & Data Deletion Request". Upon verification, all your personal memos, media files, and account credentials will be permanently purged within 30 days.

7. Children's Privacy

MemoZen does not knowingly collect or solicit personal identifiable information from children under the age of 13 (or under 16 in the European Union). If we become aware that a child has provided us with personal information, we will delete it promptly.

8. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update our Privacy Policy periodically. We will notify you of any changes by posting the new Privacy Policy on this page and updating the "Effective Date" above.

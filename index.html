3. Categories of Data Collected (Data Safety Alignment)

  • Account Identifiers: Name, email address, profile picture (via Google Sign-In or email credentials) to manage sessions, multi-device sync, and group collaboration.
  • User-Generated Content: Private diary texts, tags, habit checklists, voice recordings, and uploaded photos/videos.
  • Financial & Expense Records: MemoZen provides a personal expense tracking tool (amounts in VND and categories) for budgeting and group trip splitting. Disclaimer: Expense data is strictly manual text/number input by the user. MemoZen does NOT collect, store, or process credit card numbers, bank account numbers, or banking credentials.
  • Device & Diagnostics: Anonymous hardware model, OS version, app version, network status, and crash telemetry strictly for service stability and debugging.

4. Private Diary vs. Collaborative Journeys Boundary

MemoZen enforces a strict boundary between private and shared information:

  • Personal Diary (Only Me): Your personal daily thoughts, mood reflections, and private memos are 100% confidential. No other user or journey member can access them.
  • Collaborative Journeys: When you join a shared journey via an invite token, photos, memos, and expenses explicitly added to that journey container are visible to authorized participants of that trip.

5. Third-Party Service Providers

We partner only with essential infrastructure providers:

  • MapLibre / OpenStreetMap: Map vector rendering for journey visualization.

Path 1: Instant In-App Deletion (Recommended)

If you currently have the MemoZen app installed on your device, this is the fastest, instant method:

  1. Open MemoZen and navigate to Settings (Cài đặt) via the profile menu.
  2. Under the Account section, scroll down to the Danger Zone.
  3. Tap Delete Account (Xóa tài khoản).
  4. Confirm the security prompt by typing DELETE.
  5. Your local database will be erased immediately, active sessions revoked, and your cloud account will be purged permanently.

Path 2: Web-Based Deletion Request (For Uninstalled Users)

If you have uninstalled MemoZen, lost access to your device, or cannot log in, you can request full account deletion via our unauthenticated web intake protocol:

Step 1: Send an email directly to our Data Protection support team at support@memozen.io.

Step 2: Use the subject line: [Account Deletion Request] - MemoZen.

Step 3 (Security Requirement): The request MUST be sent from the exact email address associated with your MemoZen account. We will reply with a verification confirmation link to verify ownership. Kẻ xấu không thể giả mạo vì không thể truy cập hòm thư của bạn.
